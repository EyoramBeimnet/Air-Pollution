The city model is built from NYC's building footprints (1.08 million buildings, with roof heights), NYC street centerlines, and USGS 3DEP elevation. Building heights are exaggerated 1.5× up close and 3× for the whole-city view; terrain 3×. The Empire State Building, One World Trade Center, the Chrysler Building, the Statue of Liberty and the suspension bridges are modelled by hand. Ships and planes are illustrative, not live traffic.
